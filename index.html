Connection setup

Register a TikTok developer app, enable Login Kit, and register http://127.0.0.1:*/tiktok/callback/ as its desktop return address.

Use a server you control. It holds the developer secret and manages TikTok access. Your TikTok password is entered only on TikTok’s website.

BROADCAST CONNECTION

Set up your LIVE destination

For TikTok, link your profile in Account, then get the server URL and stream key from TikTok’s LIVE settings. A profile connection alone does not provide broadcast access.

The destination and key stay in memory for this app session. The key is cleared from this form after saving. TikTok can issue new details for every broadcast.

Streaming encoder

Checking for FFmpeg…

FFmpeg with libx264 is required and is detected automatically when installed. It runs separately from this app.

Connect your phone

Use Phone Camera to add your phone’s video to a scene. Use Phone Monitor to watch the composed studio video and audio on your phone. Camera + Monitor supports both on the same phone.

Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi. A secure phone link uses the Cloudflare helper and Internet access for the page and connection setup; video travels through an encrypted WebRTC connection. Phone monitoring works without a TikTok stream key and does not start a public LIVE.

Scan the QR code, choose a mode on your phone, then approve it below. New pairings expire after 15 minutes. Stop phone connections to end all phone access.
